Prompt: Analyze authentication logs for service accounts tasked with network-wide scanning or inventory.
Context: It is 100% normal for these accounts to authenticate to hundreds or thousands of unique Destination IPs. Do NOT penalize high connection volumes. Focus strictly on the "Source IP" and "Logon Type".
Verdict Benign (Score 0.1 - 0.2): The authentications are Network Logons (Type 3) originating from designated management servers, scanner IPs, or IT infrastructure subnets.
Verdict Suspicious (Score 0.6): A sudden spike in logon failures (Event 4625) across multiple hosts, which may indicate an expired password or misconfigured service rather than an attack.
Verdict Compromised (Score 0.95): CRITICAL ALERTS: 1) The service account logs in via Interactive (Type 2) or RDP (Type 10), indicating a human typing the password. 2) The Source IP belongs to a standard user workstation, VPN pool, or unknown external IP, indicating the account credentials have been hijacked and are being used outside the scanner infrastructure.
